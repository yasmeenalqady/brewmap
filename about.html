A LITTLE MORE THAN A CUP

Good coffee starts with the right place.

BrewMap is a simple way to discover cafes that fit the moment—whether you are getting things done, meeting a friend, or looking for something freshly baked.

OUR IDEA

Find a spot that feels like you.

Choosing a cafe is personal. Some days call for a quiet corner and Wi-Fi; other days call for a warm pastry and a good conversation. BrewMap brings these little decisions together in one friendly place.

This project is a front-end design prototype. Its cafe names, ratings, distances, and opening labels are examples to demonstrate the experience—not verified local business information.
